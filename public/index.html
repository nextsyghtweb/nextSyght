Frequently Asked

Questions clients ask us before signing up.

What does your online presence audit include?

Our audit analyzes your website speed and mobile experience, on-page SEO, Google Business Profile, local listings consistency, reviews, competitor rankings and how often AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews mention your business. You get a plain-language report with a prioritized fix list.

How much does an audit cost?

The 5-point starter audit is free and delivered within 48 hours. A full deep-dive audit with competitor analysis is a one-time paid report. If you want us to implement the fixes, we quote that separately after you see the report, so there is no pressure to commit before you know what is wrong.

How long does an audit take?

The free starter audit arrives within 48 hours. A full deep-dive audit with competitor analysis takes 5 to 7 working days. If you then choose to have us fix the issues, most improvements go live within 2 to 3 weeks.

What is AEO and why does my business need an AEO audit?

AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization, is what gets your business quoted directly inside ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and voice assistants instead of just listed as a blue link. Our AEO audit tests what these tools currently say about your business and shows exactly which pages and schema markup to fix so they quote you instead of a competitor.

Do I need an audit if I already have a website?

Yes, especially then. Most existing websites have hidden problems such as slow mobile loading, missing schema markup, inconsistent business listings or pages that AI tools cannot read. An audit shows what is working, what is losing you customers and what to fix first.

Which businesses need an audit?

Any business that wants customers to find it online: shops, cafes, clinics, advocates, coaching centres, tuition classes, salons, contractors, restaurants and professional firms. If your customers search on Google, Maps or ChatGPT before they call you, an audit applies to you.
